Levels:
* Level 1 - basic movement, see combat
* Level 2 - multi tank, intro to hostage rescue
* Level 3 - healthpack intro. choices intro. healthpacks too hard to miss :P
* Level open ground - bigger level with prev introduced mechanics. less railroaded. lets player start making choices and consider his approaches.
* Level Obstacle - teaches how obstacles work. easy and harder ways to finish it.
* Level Water - teaching water mechanics. more tanks (4) to control.
* Level Evade - teaching player a bit more about individual unit micro given all elements.
* Level Islands - more precision micro required. timing and coordination between units important.
* Level Final - path choices, either go for 1 harder area or 2 easier. pro players can try to get all. uses all mechanics introduced in the game, and good opportunities for microing
